Core Playbooks

Use these as “standard work” for common supply chain situations.

Playbook: Late Delivery (Supplier)

  1. Confirm the facts: PO, promise date, current ship date, quantities, partials, Incoterms/shipping method.
  2. Classify impact: line-down risk, customer commit risk, expedite cost exposure.
  3. Containment plan: partial shipment, substitute material, alternate supplier, internal resequence.
  4. Root cause: capacity, material shortage, scheduling, quality hold, communication failure.
  5. Corrective action: process change with owner + due date; verify next 3 POs.
  6. Document: action log + follow-up cadence (48h / weekly) until stable.
Artifacts: supplier action log, updated promise dates, lessons learned note.

Playbook: RFQ (Comparable Quotes)

  1. Send drawing/spec revision + required certs + packaging requirements.
  2. Require quote in a standard table: unit price, tooling, MOQ, lead time, freight, payment terms.
  3. Ask for alternates (material/processing) explicitly marked as alternates.
  4. Normalize: apples-to-apples comparison and risk notes.
  5. Award logic: cost + lead time + quality risk + strategic fit.
Artifacts: quote comparison sheet, award email template, supplier assumptions log.

Playbook: Supplier Scorecard Rollout

  1. Define metrics (OTD definition matters), targets, and measurement cadence.
  2. Start with top suppliers by spend or pain.
  3. Review monthly: trends, corrective actions, escalations.
  4. Close the loop: document improvements and tie to future business decisions.
Artifacts: scorecard PDF, meeting agenda, action log, quarterly supplier review deck.
