Glossary

OTD (On-Time Delivery)

Percent of POs delivered on or before the confirmed promise date. Always define: date used (dock vs receipt vs put-away) and how partials are counted.

PPV (Purchase Price Variance)

Difference between standard/expected cost and actual purchase cost. Useful but incomplete; pair with total cost (freight, quality, lead time risk).

Lead Time Variability

The spread in supplier lead time. Two suppliers with the same average lead time can require very different safety stock if one is inconsistent.

MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)

Smallest quantity a supplier will accept at quoted price. Impacts inventory, cash, storage, and obsolescence risk.

Root Cause vs Containment

Containment stops the bleeding now; corrective action prevents recurrence. Both must have owners and verification steps.
