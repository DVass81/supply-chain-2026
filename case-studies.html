Case Study Template

Use the structure below for every project so your portfolio stays consistent.

  • Problem: what happened and why it matters
  • Baseline: current performance and evidence
  • Root cause: what actually drove the issue
  • Countermeasures: what changed (process, system, controls)
  • Results: measurable outcomes
  • Standardization: how you made it repeatable

Example: Copper Bar Pull Error (Thickness Mix-Up)

Problem: Wrong thickness copper bar was pulled due to visually similar stock stored together.

Baseline: Multiple thicknesses (.187, .250, .312, .375, .500) stored in a shared rack without robust controls.

Root cause: Weak point-of-use identification (no hard separation, no forced verification step).

Countermeasures:

  • Hard segregation by thickness (dedicated slots/racks, physical dividers).
  • Large-font labels + color bands + “thickness gauge” go/no-go check at pull.
  • Pick verification: second-person check or barcode scan (if feasible).

Results: Reduced mis-pulls; improved pull speed and confidence.

Standardization: Updated work instruction + training + audit checklist.
